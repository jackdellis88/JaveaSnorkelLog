How the Snorkel Log works

🗺️ Ship's Log — your home screen. See how many species you've spotted, check today's Snorkel-o-Meter (a live rating of sea conditions off Jávea), and see the featured Card of the Day.

📖 Card Collection — browse all 50 creatures as collectible cards. Search by name, filter by rarity, category or collector, and toggle between all cards, caught only, or not yet caught. Tap a card to open a full-view swipeable gallery — swipe or use the arrows to move between cards, then tap "More Info" for extra detail or "Log a Sighting" to record a find.

🖼️ Card photos — tap any species photo to enlarge it full-screen.

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Profile — add up to 6 collector names, and choose who's using the device right now.

💾 Backups — your sightings are saved in this browser only. Use Export/Import on the Ship's Log tab to back up or move your data to another device.
